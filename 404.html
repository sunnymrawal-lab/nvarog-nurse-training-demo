Three workers, three journeys, one certification engine

Each of these is the real thing: the actual course content, the actual exam questions, and live scoring. Pick a role and walk the whole journey — from the first message to a certificate a family or a hospital can check.

All three take the same ten core modules, then a role add-on — which is why the ICU nurse's twenty modules cost only six more than the nurse's fourteen. Where a lesson sits outside a role's scope of practice, that role never sees it. That rule is enforced in the data, not in the copy.
